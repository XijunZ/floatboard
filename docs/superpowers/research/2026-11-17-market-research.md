# Market research: personal CRM, notes, decisions, plans (17 Nov 2026)

Evidence is mostly vendor blogs and reviews; few user forums. Key points:

Validated: tiers + intervals (a gap in Dex/Covve/Monica; fixed generic reminders get ignored — goodword.com);
automatic last-contact (manual logging is why Monica fails; Mesh/Clay and Dex read Gmail, Calendar, WhatsApp);
AI cleanup must keep the verbatim text (AudioPen's top complaint); tags over folders (Mem zero-filing);
30/90/180-day decision reviews (common templates; failure = forgetting to review, over-complex entries);
goals linked to weekly work, lightly (Sunsama weekly objectives).

Warnings: too much structure in contacts (Monica HN thread); wrong last-contact dates destroy trust; enrichment is a
paid-data business; decisions found after the fact lack the "before" picture (expected outcome, confidence), so the
select-text form is the stronger route; many areas dilute the core (UpHabit closed its personal CRM apps Apr 2026 after
drifting into sales tooling).

Worth taking: verbatim + cleanup; a "due this week" list instead of per-contact alerts; auto timeline from email and
calendar; one-tap "I contacted them" and snooze; today's events on top of the daily jots; tags suggested after capture;
4-field decision form (decision, why, expected outcome, confidence); review opens the original with the prediction beside
it; ~3 goals a quarter that tasks tick against, no scoring; context triggers (birthday, meeting with them next week);
compact Today / Rest of week / Next week phone summary.
